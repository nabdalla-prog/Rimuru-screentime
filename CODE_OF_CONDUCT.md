# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want their computer to tell them where the time went, and for the people who help make that better. Everyone taking part is expected to:

- be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people whose first language isn't English;
- assume good intent, and disagree with ideas rather than people;
- keep discussion on topic and free of harassment, insults and discrimination of any kind;
- respect other people's privacy, and never post someone else's personal data (including their screen time).

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the address on their GitHub profile, or use a private security advisory if it involves safety.
